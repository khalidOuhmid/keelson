# Gaps

Anything noticed **outside the scope of the current milestone** is recorded here, never handled in
the current milestone. One row per finding.

Status as of 2026-11-11: every milestone in the plan has shipped. Proposals that point to a
milestone L5 to L8 without a **CLOSED** mark were not handled there and remain open: they form the
backlog after v0.1.0.
